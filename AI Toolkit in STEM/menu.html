AI Toolkit for STEM Education

The following resource compiles a range of AI tools that are useful in the Department of Engineering. The aim of this toolkit is to have a collaborative space where colleagues can share best practices and state-of-the-art AI tools. Work in Progress.

Key Issues for AI

  • 🔓 Open
    Transparency or how much information they provide about their algorithms, training data, etc…
  • 🕵️ Private
    The way they process the information you use as input in their service.
  • ✔️ Validity
    How accurate or reliable are the outputs of the service.
  • ⚖️ Non-biased
    Encompasses digital poverty, accessibility, EDI, etc…
  • 🛡️ Ethical
    Encompasses a wide range of topics. For instance, how was training data sourced? the case of translators, actors, graphic designers, etc…. Another example is carbon emissions for minimal improvements

Since the rapid development of AI outpaces the implementation of effective regulations, these Guiding Principles have been proposed to inform the adoption of AI tools:
Know what AI is and its limitations
Use AI lawfully, ethically and responsibly
Use AI securely
Meaningful human controls at the right stage
Proper management of AI lifecycle
Use the right tool for the job
Open and collaborative
Commercial colleagues from start
Skills and expertise to implement AI
Right assurances in place
