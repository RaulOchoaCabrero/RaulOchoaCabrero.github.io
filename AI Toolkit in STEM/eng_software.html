Fusion 360 Generative Design

Consensus is an AI search engine for research. According to their websit, it responsibly uses AI to help conduct research faster. These are the main points:

  • Extensive research coverage, earch through over 200M research papers across every domain of science and academia.
  • Time-saving AI insights, gain insight faster with our Pro Analysis and Consensus Meter. We leverage both OpenAI and custom LLMs.
  • Find the most relevant papers, our proprietary academic search tools and filters help you find the most relevant and reliable research papers, faster.
  • Results connected to science, everything we show in our product is cited. You're always only one click away from the underlying research paper.

This is an example of the of outputs of the AI tool. The search inquries about the effect of exercise and cognition and the AI generates a synthesis of the research on the topic (proprietary LLM) with an bar visualising the consesus on the question and a conclusion at the end. It also suggests related questions and the results provide a list of the peer-reviewed articles and sources that are used to provide the information. They have tags such as "Meta Analysis" or "Very Rigorous Journal" to help the reader filter through the research papers on the topic.
