Large Language Models

Copilot 🕵️✔️

Copilot is the LLM institutionally supported by Manchester Met. It has been internally vetted and the data you provide is privat, additionally students have free access using their Microsoft account.   

  • Based on Chat- GPT 4, industry standard tool for conversational AI assistance.
  • Integrated Bing Search, improved accuracy with web search integration.
  • Integrated Image Generation (DALL-E), includes access to image generation capabilities of DALL-E (although Copilot prompts DALL-E for you!).
  • Image Understanding, capable of understanding the content of images and provide context about the.
  • Integration with Microsoft Products, works seamlessly with other Microsoft applicaitons like Word and Teams.

Most computers with Windows 11 have Copilot included, you can also access using the Edge browser (to- right corner) or any browser by typing the web address (below). The image helps understand the interface and how to interact with Copilot. There are numerous resources centred around the effective use of generative AI tools, particularly LLMs. A link to the Rise study pack is included below and a brief overview of Prompt Engineering (the methodology to get reliable and consistent outputs) are included to get you started. The use of AI in Higher Education is a vast topic and we encourage you to engage with it all aspects of it, not just the effective use of LLMs. Ethical considerations and critical evaluation are key aspects of using any AI tool.
